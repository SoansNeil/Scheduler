What do these times mean?
Completion time
The time on the schedule when a process finishes.
Turnaround time
Total time from a process arriving until it finishes: completion time − arrival time.
Waiting time
Total time a process spends waiting for the CPU: turnaround time − burst time.
Response time
Time from arrival until a process first runs on the CPU.
